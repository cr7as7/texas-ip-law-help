Patent basics for inventors

Educational explainer by Texas IP Law Group. Not legal advice for your facts.

A patent is a time-limited right to exclude others from making, using, selling, or importing what the claims cover. It is not a permission slip to commercialize, and it is not the same as a trademark or copyright.

Before you file

  • Write a dated description and sketches of how the invention works — not just that it exists.
  • List everyone who contributed to the inventive idea (inventorship is a legal question).
  • Note every public disclosure: pitches, demos, papers, Kickstarter, GitHub.
  • Read the USPTO patent basics so the forms make sense.
