Licensing your IP

Educational explainer by Texas IP Law Group. Not legal advice for your facts.

A license lets someone else use your IP on terms you set. Assignment transfers ownership. Mixing those up in an email to a manufacturer is a common way inventors lose control.

Terms to recognize: exclusive vs. non-exclusive, field of use, territory, royalties vs. lump sum, improvements, and who enforces against copycats. This is not a contract template.

If someone sent you a term sheet, that is a consultation moment — not an AI moment.
