Is my idea patentable?

Educational explainer by Texas IP Law Group. Not legal advice for your facts.

U.S. utility patents generally require patent-eligible subject matter, novelty, and non-obviousness, plus an enabling description. Whether your idea qualifies is a legal and technical judgment — the AI and this article will not declare it patentable.

At a high level: if the public already has the same invention, or if it would have been obvious to a skilled person in view of what already exists, a patent is unlikely. Abstract ideas, laws of nature, and natural phenomena have extra eligibility issues.

A prior-art search is a start; an attorney opinion is different. See the patent guide or ask a general question.
