Frequently asked questions

Short answers. Guides and official sites have the steps. None of this is legal advice for your facts.

Is this the USPTO?

No. Texas IP Law Help is an educational site by Texas IP Law Group. Filing happens on uspto.gov or copyright.gov.

Patent, trademark, or copyright — which one?

Invention / how it works → patent. Name or logo you sell under → trademark. Writing, art, music, code as expression → copyright. Many businesses need more than one.

Can I file myself?

The USPTO allows it. Forms, fees, and claims are easy to get wrong. Use our guides to reach the official pages; use a consultation if the invention, a deadline, or investors are in the mix.

Does the AI give legal advice?

No. It explains general concepts and links to guides and USPTO pages. See the disclaimer.

I cannot afford a lawyer. What then?

See official resources for the Inventors Assistance Center and TALA Patent Pro Bono (income limits, waitlist). We do not operate those programs.

How do I hire Texas IP Law Group?

Contact or use Free consultation. The firm site is texasiplawgroup.com.
