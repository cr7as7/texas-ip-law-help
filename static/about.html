About Texas IP Law Help

Texas IP Law Help (texasiplawhelp.com) is a full inventor website: start-here paths, step-by-step guides, articles, official USPTO and Copyright Office links, FAQ, search, and an educational AI demo.

It is operated by Texas IP Law Group, a patent and IP law firm. This site is separate from the firm website so you can learn and reach government forms without wading through attorney sales pages first.

What we are not

We are not a government agency, not TexasLawHelp, and not legal aid. AI and guides are not legal advice and do not create an attorney-client relationship. See the disclaimer.
